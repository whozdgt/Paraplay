Terms of use

Last updated 4 November 2026.

Paraplay plays music you already have. These terms cover the optional account and the things other people can see: your name, bio, pictures, shared playlists and what you're listening to.

No tolerance for abuse

Don't post anything abusive, hateful, harassing, sexual, violent, illegal, or that impersonates someone. This covers usernames, display names, bios, pictures and playlist names. There is zero tolerance: content like this is removed, and accounts that post it are suspended or deleted.

Reporting and blocking

Every profile has Report and Block. A blocked person can no longer see you or follow you. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours, and the content and account are removed where they break these terms.

Your music

Paraplay doesn't host, sell or share music. You're responsible for having the right to play the files and servers you use with it.

Your account

Keep your password safe: there is no password reset. You can delete your account at any time, in the app or on the delete your account page. How your data is handled is in the privacy policy.

No warranty

Paraplay is free, open-source software provided as it is, without warranty of any kind, as described in its licence. The account service may change or stop.

Open an issue on GitHub, or use the contact address on the app's store page.
