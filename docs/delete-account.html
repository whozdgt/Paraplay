Delete your Paraplay account

This deletes your account and everything stored with it, straight away: your profile, profile picture and banner, playlists saved to your account, follows, blocks, reports and listening status. Nothing is kept afterwards. Music files on your devices are not touched.

You can also do this in the app: open your profile and choose Delete account.

Can't sign in?

There is no password reset. Open an issue on GitHub titled "Delete my account" with your username only (never your password), or use the contact address on the app's store page. You may be asked to show that the account is yours, for example from a device where you are still signed in. The account is then deleted within 7 days.

Your username and password go straight to Paraplay's account service (Supabase) and are not stored by this page.
